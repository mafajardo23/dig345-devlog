##### A summary of the content

During the video “Why is Everything Binary?” the video explains the important yet extremely system foundation for computers. Transistors, bits and bytes, and the binary system. This system is how computers operate and it comes down to the simplest terms, 1 or 0. High electrical impulses or low. That’s it. However, the video mentions how this binary system can be applied to us as well as binary thinking. When we think its one thing or the other. For instance, people use either their right brain more than their left.

##### Quote from the content
- “Humans set to be using binary thinking are not maximizing their capacity… in humans it means thinking with just one bit, representing one of two possible states.”

##### Related comment on the content
- I believe the author is making an really interesting critique of how uptight and black and white humans are. He brings an important perspective in terms of our society, specially nowadays. 

##### Related discussion question (a prompt you would like to explore in class)
- What are some ways we could stop binary thinking and what would be the benefits?
